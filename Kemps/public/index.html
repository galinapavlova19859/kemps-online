Kemps

Collect four of a kind. Signal your partner. Don't get caught.

or join one
How to play
  • Teams of two; partners sit opposite each other.
  • Everyone holds 4 cards. There are 4 cards face up in the middle. There are no turns: swap a card from your hand with one in the middle whenever you want.
  • Before the game, each team picks a secret signal emote. Only your team can see which emote is yours.
  • Anyone can send any emote at any time, so you can bluff.
  • When you have four of a kind, send your signal. Your partner then hits KEMPS! for a point. A wrong call gives the point away.
  • Think an opponent has four of a kind? Hit Counter on them. If you're right, your team scores. If you're wrong, theirs does.
  • When the middle goes stale, everyone can vote to refresh it.
